Privacy Policy

Cloud Games · Last updated 2026-10-16

Cloud Games is made by Digital Magnolia (Baton Rouge, Louisiana, USA). This policy explains, in plain English, what the app collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. In short: we collect what the app needs to work and to stay safe. We do not run ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in with an emailed link or with Sign in with Apple. If you use Hide My Email, we only receive Apple's relay address. We do not store your name from Apple.
  • Your profile: your username, an optional display name, and when you confirmed you are 13 or older.
  • The skies you post: the photo, and whatever you add to it — title, story, category, cloud shape, feeling, tag, Sky Scene narration, and "who you watched it with". If your photo records the date it was taken, we save that date.
  • What you do with other members' skies: your "I see it too" approvals, loves, follows, blocks, and reports.
  • Safety-review results for your posts and profile (see "Automated safety review, including AI").
  • Only if you turn them on: a push-notification token for your device, and for Golden Hour, a city-level area and your time zone (see "Location").

We do not collect your contacts, the rest of your photo library, health data, or payment details.

Your photos

Cloud Games only uses the photo you choose to share. Before upload, the app makes three resized copies and re-encodes them, which strips the hidden photo metadata (EXIF) — including any GPS location — from the files we store.

Shared skies can be seen by other signed-in members. Their image files are served from unlisted web links so they load quickly.

Skies you keep "Just for me" are stored separately and shown only to you in the app. Like every post, they still go through the automated safety review, and if one is held, a moderator may review it.

Location: off unless you turn it on

Cloud Games does not need your location to post. A sky only uses location if you switch on "Add this sky to the Atlas" for that sky and the photo itself has a location. Then:

  • Your phone asks Apple's map service for the city name of the photo's location.
  • We store the city, state or region, and country, plus coordinates rounded to about 11 km. We never store the precise coordinates.
  • To place your sky on the World Sky Map, the precise coordinates are sent once to our server, which looks for a known public place nearby (a viewpoint, landmark, or town from open map data). If it finds one, your sky is shown at that public place. The coordinates are used only for that lookup and are not saved.

Because an Atlas sky is shown at a public place near where it was taken, people who see it can tell you were near that place. If you leave the switch off, no location is read, sent, or stored for that sky.

Golden Hour (optional, in the Notification Garden): when you turn it on, the app asks for your location once, at low accuracy, rounds it to about 11 km, and saves that area and your time zone to your profile so we can time a sunset reminder. To look up sunset times, our server sends only the rounded area — never who you are — to sunrise-sunset.org. This area is not shown to other people in the app. Turning Golden Hour off stops the reminders; to erase the saved area, delete your account or email us.

Automated safety review, including AI

Every sky you post — shared or "Just for me" — and your username and display name are checked automatically before they appear:

  • OpenAI's moderation service checks photos and text for things like sexual content, violence, and hate.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads text only, to flag words that seem unkind.
  • Sightengine checks a photo to estimate whether it was AI-generated. Skies near the top of the leaderboards may be checked again.

These services receive the photo (through a web link) or the words — never your email address. For "Just for me" skies, the link is private and expires after two minutes. We store the results, such as approved, held, or removed, and an AI-image score. Anything uncertain is held for a person on our team to review and is not shown publicly; content the check is highly confident breaks our rules is removed automatically.

You agree to this review when you create your profile. It is required: we cannot let content appear without it.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in, and photo storage, hosted in the United States.
  • Resend — sends sign-in emails.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push-notification delivery, maps, and city names for Atlas skies.
  • Expo — push-notification delivery and app updates.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, and Sightengine — automated safety review (above).
  • sunrise-sunset.org — sunset times for Golden Hour (receives a rounded area only).

Each receives only what its job needs and processes it to provide that service to us, under its own business terms and privacy commitments.

What we never do

No advertising. No selling or renting your data. No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites, so there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt. No analytics, advertising, or data-broker SDKs in the app. We never store precise location.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and stay off until you turn them on in the Notification Garden. You will get at most two a day, and never marketing. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Who can see what

Other signed-in members can see your username and display name, your shared skies and what you wrote with them (including "who you watched it with"), the city, region, and country of skies you added to the Atlas, how many approvals and loves your skies receive, and your place on the leaderboards. People you block cannot see your profile or skies, and you cannot see theirs. Our moderators can see content when they need to review it.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists.

Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, profile, every sky (shared and private), approvals, loves, follows, reports, blocks, notification settings, and push tokens. Photo files are deleted in the same step; occasionally a file can take a short while longer to be fully removed from storage. We do not keep backups of deleted data, so it cannot be recovered. Anything already sent to a provider for a safety check is kept only under that provider's own retention terms, and our providers' system logs expire on their own schedules.

Deleting a single sky is not available in the app yet. Email us and we will remove it for you.

Children

Cloud Games is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can see your skies in your Journal, turn location and notifications on or off, and delete everything by deleting your account. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or the removal of something specific, email regardforbeauregard@gmail.com from the address on your account. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights (such as access, correction, portability, and erasure); we will honor them as the law requires. We do not sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your data is transferred there.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and let you know in the app before the change takes effect. Questions or requests: regardforbeauregard@gmail.com.
