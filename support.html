Support

We’re here to help. Most answers are below; for anything else, email regardforbeauregard@gmail.com.

Questions

What is Cloud Games?

A free iPhone app for the skies you find: shapes in the clouds, sunrises, sunsets, golden hours. Share a sky with the community or keep it "Just for me" in your private Journal. Members say "I see it too", and the best-loved skies earn crowns, Sky of the Day, and a place in the weekly Winners Circle.

How do I sign in?

Use Continue with Apple, or enter your email and tap the sign-in link we send you. There is no password. If the email doesn’t arrive in a few minutes, check spam or junk, or use Continue with Apple.

Who can use it?

Anyone 13 or older. You confirm your age when you create your profile.

Why was my sky "in gentle review"?

Every photo and caption is checked automatically before it appears (see the Privacy Policy). Most skies appear within seconds. If the check is unsure, a person on our team reviews it first, and some skies never appear if they don’t fit the Community Guidelines.

Is my location shared?

Only if you switch on "Add this sky to the Atlas" for a sky, and only if that photo has a location. We store the city and an area rounded to about 11 km — never your precise location. Leave the switch off and nothing about location is used. Details.

Are "Just for me" skies private?

Yes. Only you see them in the app. Like every sky, they still pass the automatic safety check.

How do I report a sky or block someone?

Tap the "⋯" menu on the sky, then choose Report or Block. Blocking takes effect immediately for both of you. To report a username, or to undo a block, email us.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Journal → the bell icon (Notification Garden). Everything there is optional; you get at most two notifications a day, and never marketing.

Can I delete one of my skies?

Not in the app yet. Email us from your account’s address with the sky’s title or date and we will remove it.

Does it cost anything?

No. Cloud Games is free, with no ads and no purchases.

Delete your account

  1. Open Cloud Games and go to the Journal tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

This permanently deletes your account, profile, and every sky you posted — shared and private — along with your approvals, loves, follows, and notification settings. It cannot be undone. Signed in with Apple? You can also stop using Apple ID with the app in iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Can’t get into the app? Email regardforbeauregard@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you.

Contact

Email regardforbeauregard@gmail.com. Include your username and, if it’s about a sky, its title or date. We read every message.
