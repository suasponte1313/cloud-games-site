Who can use Cloud Games

You must be 13 or older. You confirm this when you create your profile. If you are under the age where you can agree to these terms yourself where you live, a parent or guardian must agree to them for you.

One account per person. Keep your email account or Apple ID secure — anyone who can sign in to it can sign in to your account.

Your skies stay yours

By posting, you give us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to store, copy, resize, display, and distribute that content inside Cloud Games, and to have our service providers process it (including the automated safety review described below). This license exists only so the app can work, and it ends when the content is deleted — when you delete your account, or when we remove it.

Skies you keep "Just for me" are shown only to you in the app.

Only post what is yours to share: photos you took and words you wrote. Do not post anything that infringes someone else's rights.

Automated safety review

To keep Cloud Games safe, every photo and every piece of text you post — including "Just for me" skies — and your username and display name are checked automatically by third-party services, some of which use AI (OpenAI, Anthropic, and Sightengine). Content may be approved, held for a person on our team to review, or removed. You agree to this review when you create your profile; posting is not possible without it. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is sent and why.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abuse

There is no place here for objectionable content or abusive behavior. That includes harassment, bullying, hate, threats, sexual or explicit material, violence or gore, anything that exploits or endangers minors, spam, scams, and impersonation.

We have zero tolerance for it. We may remove such content and suspend or permanently end the accounts that post it, at any time and without notice.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a sky or block the person who posted it from the "⋯" menu on that sky. Reports go to our moderation team; we review them and act on anything that breaks these terms or the Community Guidelines. Blocking takes effect immediately: you stop seeing that person's skies and profile, and they stop seeing yours.

To report something that has no menu (a username, for example), or to undo a block, email regardforbeauregard@gmail.com.

Community Guidelines

One rule sits above all the others here: if something could make two people argue or one person feel small, it does not belong. Everything shared should be joyful, hopeful, gentle, and beautiful.

What belongs here

Skies you found yourself — sunrises, storms rolling in, golden hours, shapes in the clouds, the moon through mist — and the words they stir in you. Kind approvals of other people’s finds. That’s the whole game.

What never belongs here — zero tolerance

Harassment, bullying, hate, or threats toward anyone. Sexual or explicit content. Violence or gore. Anything that exploits or endangers minors. Spam, scams, or impersonation. Photos that are not yours, or that expose someone else’s private life without their consent. We remove it, and accounts that post it can be suspended or ended without warning.

Real skies

Share photos you took. AI-generated or faked skies are not eligible for crowns or awards, and may be held or removed.

People in your photos

Skies are the subject here. If a person can be recognized in your photo, make sure they would be glad to be in it. Never post someone’s image to mock, expose, or harm them.

If you see something wrong

Report it: open the "⋯" menu on the sky and choose Report — it takes seconds. Our moderation team reviews reports. You can also block the person from the same menu; blocking takes effect immediately and hides their skies from you and yours from them. For anything without a menu, email regardforbeauregard@gmail.com.

What happens when the rules are broken

Every post is checked automatically before it appears. Posts that might not fit are held for a person to review and never appear if they don’t belong. Content that crosses the zero-tolerance line is removed, and the account behind it can be suspended or permanently ended. We would rather the sky stay small and kind than large and cruel.
